about

The person behind the marks.

Hi, I'm Grace (Linge Guo).

I'm a trained sociologist studying the societal impact of digital technologies through a sociotechnical lens. With a particular interest in AI, I examine the underlying mechanisms of its infrastructure to inform ethics from a sociological perspective.

I studied Sociology at UCL, then completed my MSc in Sociology at the University of Oxford — deepening my technical grounding at the Oxford Internet Institute in Fairness, Transparency, and Accountability of Machine Learning.

Currently I'm working at the intersection of AI companions, AI deception, and platform governance — open my research threads →
